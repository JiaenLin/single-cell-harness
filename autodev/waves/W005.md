## The change

**The command.** `scrnaseq-lab convert` becomes a package bin with explicit role flags:
`--cluster --sample --condition --extra` (repeatable), `--interaction a,b`,
`--embedding --label --sentinel`.
- It guesses a role only when that role's flag is absent, and it prints every guess.
- Exit codes say what failed.

**What conversion carries.**
- Conversion carries every design factor, and notes any factor it does not carry (P11).
- It reads anndata's default nullable-string encoding (P13).
- It writes a byte-reproducible zip: entry times are fixed, and `collection.json` lists a sha256 for each entry (P16).

**Out of scope.** The browser app's behaviour does not change; the browser keeps "nothing uploaded".
