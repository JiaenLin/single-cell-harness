# W008 (PLAT): Studio says only what it does, and keeps sentinels out of the cell types

**Registered** 2026-11-09 by the coordinator, before any number. **Gaps** GAP-P22, GAP-P23, GAP-P12
(Studio's half). **Goals** A2.2, PLAT.3, A6.2. **Repository** scrnaseq-studio, base `main` = `92ef97f`,
branch `autodev/W008-studio-truth`.

## The change
- **P22.** In pseudobulk mode the README, the Overview copy and the generated Methods paragraph say what
  happens: summed counts are exported for DESeq2, and no test is run here. They no longer say
  "tested with DESeq2". The Methods paragraph is the text users copy into papers.
- **P23.** DESIGN.md sections are marked shipped or planned. The README numbers are re-measured with the
  existing `scripts/bench-bundle.mjs`.
- **P12, Studio's half.** Cells whose cluster is a sentinel (the bundle's `sentinels`, as Lab's
  `autodev/W005-lab-cli` writes them; read that branch for the field) are drawn grey. They are left out
  of denominators, legends, and marker and DE populations. Without `sentinels`, behaviour is unchanged.

## Acceptance check
- A Methods render in pseudobulk mode asserts that "tested with DESeq2" is absent.
- A bundle carrying sentinels gives composition and legends without EXCLUDED or UNRESOLVED.
- The tests fail on `92ef97f`, and `npm test` and the build are green.
- A resample of 10 doc claims finds none false.
- The browser keeps "nothing uploaded".

## Prediction
1. The checks that existed at `92ef97f` (1,512) still pass.
2. Each new test fails on the base.
3. A bundle without `sentinels` renders exactly as before. Its composition numbers are unchanged.
