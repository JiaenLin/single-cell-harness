## The protocol (RULES tier II, the maker round's rules)
- `de` changes ONLY through the maker. A plugin is maker output; no hand edit of `kernels/de*`.
- **Blind first.** Run the maker on `de` as it stands, and record what it produces and what the dev suite
  says, BEFORE changing the maker. That record is the test of the maker's generality.
- Only then fix what the maker lacks, in the maker or in the worksheet answers, never in the kernel, and
  regenerate. A maker fix found here is tested on the next held-out plugin (abundance), not only on `de`.

## The change
- `de` moves into the maker's layout and passes `sch dev` and `sch conform`.
- **GAP-039:** DE contrasts each treatment against its DECLARED control, not the alphabetically first
  level. The PI's report, slide 15, has "Aged vs Young | Chow" and "HFD vs Chow | Young".
- **GAP-040, DE's part:** a covariate is modelled, or refused by name.

## Acceptance check
- The blind record is written before any maker change.
- `de` passes the dev ladder.
- The tests show the declared-control direction and the covariate behaviour.
- `git diff f5175ea -- kernels/` holds only maker-generated output; the maker's own record shows it.
