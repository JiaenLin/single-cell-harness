## The change
Job `26_promoted_bd45f5d_reproduce.pbs` re-runs the promoted run's commands at `bd45f5d` on today's
`results/01_qc/objects`. It records the environment (python and package versions), and compares
EVERY obs column of every object it writes with the promoted tree, by barcode, in both directions,
using job 25's rule:
- strings, categories, ints and bools are exact;
- floats are equal within max(1e-9 relative, 1e-12 absolute);
- NaN equals NaN;
- a change of dtype or of categories counts as a difference.

It also compares the joint object, the scope record, and every table, with byte differences named.
The coordinator moves `~/tools/scAnno` to `bd45f5d` (with HEAD.txt) only for this job, and back
afterwards.

prediction as written, and a miss is recorded as a miss. Nothing is promoted: promotion is the PI's.
