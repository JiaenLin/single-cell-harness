- asw_batch, asw_label and isolated_labels_asw agree to about 1e-6 in all three, and X_harmony's max
  |diff| prints 0.0000 (to 4 decimals).
- For `none` and `bbknn`, every scIB metric except iLISI/cLISI agrees within 1e-6 between runs (job 28).

The variation is therefore in what is computed FROM harmony's embedding by discrete steps (the
neighbour graph, the clustering behind ARI/NMI and isolated-label F1), and it predates W012.

## The change
1. **Find the source, measured, before any fix.** Candidates:
   - harmony's threaded arithmetic giving bit-level X_harmony differences (BLAS/OpenMP thread counts);
   - the neighbour search on X_harmony (approximate or parallel nearest neighbours, its seed);
   - the clustering seed or optimisation in scIB's clustering metrics;
   - the 2,086 withheld rows.

   Each is tested in isolation on a synthetic object large enough to show it. A test demonstrates the
   cause.
2. **Remove the variation at its source,** the step that is nondeterministic, with the cause named in
   the code and the docs. Pinning a thread count or a seed is a fix only if the test shows that it
   removes the variation. Loosening a tolerance is not a fix.
3. **If a residual variation cannot be removed,** it is measured over N runs on the cluster, and that
   measured spread is named in the report, the way the LISI spread is.

## Acceptance check
- The test shows the cause and fails on `69a54b2`.
- Two runs of harmony plus scIB at one commit, on the synthetic object, give every metric within 1e-6.
- The suite is green, and the leak guard and conform are clean with both cohort lists.
- **Then one re-gate job** for W012 and W025, written before it runs. It reproduces stage 3 against the
  promoted run, with harmony's lines stated from what this wave establishes. It may be graded against
  the promoted run's numbers only where those are shown to be reproducible.

## Prediction
The builder writes it per cause, before code. The coordinator's prior is that bit-level X_harmony
differences from threaded arithmetic flip near-tie neighbours. If so, two runs with a fixed thread
count give identical X_harmony bits, and identical metrics.
