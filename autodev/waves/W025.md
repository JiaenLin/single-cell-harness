## The change
1. **Find the source, measured, before any fix.** Candidates:
   - harmony's threaded arithmetic giving bit-level X_harmony differences (BLAS/OpenMP thread counts);
   - the neighbour search on X_harmony (approximate or parallel nearest neighbours, its seed);
   - the clustering seed or optimisation in scIB's clustering metrics;
   - the 2,086 withheld rows.

   Each is tested in isolation on a synthetic object large enough to show it. A test demonstrates the
   cause.
2. **Remove the variation at its source,** the step that is nondeterministic, with the cause named in
   the code and the docs. Pinning a thread count or a seed is a fix only if the test shows that it
   removes the variation. Loosening a tolerance is not a fix.
3. **If a residual variation cannot be removed,** it is measured over N runs on the cluster, and that
   measured spread is named in the report, the way the LISI spread is.

## Acceptance check

**The choice: score every cell. Why, by what scIB's helper allows and by the cost.**
- The helper takes no seed. `knn_graph.cpp` accepts exactly five arguments and seeds `mt19937 gen(rd())` from
  `std::random_device`; it skips a cell when a draw from 1 to 100 exceeds the percentage. At 100 no draw can skip a cell,
  so the result does not depend on the draw: every-cell LISI is deterministic with scIB as shipped, no patch.
- A seeded subsample is possible only if the tool takes the draw over: run the helper on every cell (it cannot be told
  which), then pick a seeded half before scIB's Simpson step, i.e. replace scIB's own glue (`lisi_graph_py`). That keeps
  the helper's full cost and saves only half the Simpson step: about 6 s of 27 s per metric at 10^5 cells. Not worth a
  reimplementation of scIB's call path.
- Every cell costs +11 s per metric at 10^5 cells on one core. SAMBO's job makes 28 LISI calls (5 methods x 2 metrics x
  2 label passes, plus 2 x 2 x 2 in the repeat): about 5 minutes more on one core, less where `--n-cores` spreads the
  Simpson step, against a job of hours. A 4-core run at 10^5 cells is being measured; its number goes in the close.
