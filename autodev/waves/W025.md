## The change
1. **Find the source, measured, before any fix.** Candidates:
   - harmony's threaded arithmetic giving bit-level X_harmony differences (BLAS/OpenMP thread counts);
   - the neighbour search on X_harmony (approximate or parallel nearest neighbours, its seed);
   - the clustering seed or optimisation in scIB's clustering metrics;
   - the 2,086 withheld rows.

   Each is tested in isolation on a synthetic object large enough to show it. A test demonstrates the
   cause.
2. **Remove the variation at its source,** the step that is nondeterministic, with the cause named in
   the code and the docs. Pinning a thread count or a seed is a fix only if the test shows that it
   removes the variation. Loosening a tolerance is not a fix.
3. **If a residual variation cannot be removed,** it is measured over N runs on the cluster, and that
   measured spread is named in the report, the way the LISI spread is.
