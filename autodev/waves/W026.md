stage through the kernel, and stage 2 is next.

## The change
- **An `anno@scanno` adapter**, in the Universal Plugin Format of ADR-0030 and following W010's
  `qc@scqc` pattern. It calls scAnno's REAL CLI through the kernel, with the commands and order of
  SAMBO's stage-2 chain (cluster, annotate per sample, scope, annotate --scope, rescue) as job 25
  runs them. It takes stage 1's deliverable as its observation, read-only and pinned (E2).
- **Labels are layers, not removals.** The adapter adds scAnno's label columns as a reversible layer.
  It never edits the observation. Unmounting restores the pre-mount digest, as for `qc@scqc`.
- **No assay default (rule 2).** The adapter passes the assay declared for the run. scAnno refuses a
  mixed or unknown assay itself (W011).
