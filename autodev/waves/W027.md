# W027 (records): choose the scale cohort for A3.4

**Registered** 2026-11-09 by the coordinator, before any search. **Gaps** GAP-016. **Goals** A3.4, G3.
**No repository changes.** A research agent proposes a cohort, and nothing is downloaded. Choosing a
public cohort is a design decision under the pre-decider, not the PI's (gap review, table A7). The
coordinator decides from the proposal and reports the choice to the PI.

## Why now
A3.4 asks for at least 1,000,000 cells through A1.1. It is FAIL, and no cohort is chosen. Staging a
dataset of that size is the slowest step: download, transfer, checksums, building a design sheet. So it
starts while the harness gains its adapters.

## What the cohort must have
- **At least 1,000,000 cells or nuclei,** with RAW counts (not only normalised values), public, with a
  licence that allows research reuse.
- **A declared assay** (rule 2: single cells or single nuclei), never a mixture without a column that
  says which.
- **A design:** at least one factor with a declared control, replicates (samples or donors) per arm, and
  ideally a second factor or a covariate.
- **Formats the tools read:** h5ad, 10x MEX or h5, or per-sample matrices.
- **A species the family supports,** human or mouse.
- **Not SAMBO's or C46's study,** to keep the held-out rule.
- **Cell-type labels from the authors,** so annotation can be checked. Not required.

## Deliverable
`autodev/scale/CANDIDATES.md` (written into the harness by the coordinator, from the agent's report). It
holds three candidates, ranked, each with:
- accession and URL; licence;
- cells and samples; assay; species; tissue;
- design factors and controls; files, formats and total size;
- whether raw counts are present (measured from the repository's metadata, not assumed);
- known issues.

It also gives the reasons for the ranking.

## Acceptance check
The coordinator checks every claimed number against the source page, by a second read. The choice is
recorded in STATE's "Decided" table with the rule that decided it. Downloading and staging are a later
wave, under the HPC layout's transfer rules.

## Prediction
There is at least one public candidate of 1M+ cells with raw counts, a declared assay and a
two-arm-or-more design: CELLxGENE Census or a large atlas collection. The prior is that most 1M+
collections are atlases without a controlled design, so finding a DESIGNED one is the hard part.
