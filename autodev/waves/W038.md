   imports.

   model is the job that did this for scQC (PBS 783151) and scProfile (PBS 782609); their job files are in the same
   cohort's stage folders.

   `var['gene_symbol']`, so on scQC's deliverable every marker reads as unmeasurable. One reader of an object's gene
   symbols for all three commands, with a test that fails on `68233f0`.
