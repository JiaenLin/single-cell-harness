# W044 (ANNO): the proposed tree keeps to the tissue, and scAnno carries no cohort string

**Registered** 2026-11-11 by the coordinator, before any code or number. ROUTE items R17 and R16's second run. **Gaps**
GAP-C85, scAnno's known leak hits. **Goals** ANNO.1, A3.1, A1.1. **Repository** github.com/JiaenLin/scAnno, branch
`autodev/W044-tree-and-leaks`, from `main` (`779fd08`, W038 merged 2026-11-11).

## Why
1. **GAP-C85.** `scanno tree propose` for Human / Brain drafts immune, tumour and endothelial lineages beside the neural
   ones and files "Proliferating cell" under B cell. On the second cohort (brain organoids, 53,534 cells after QC, PBS
   784490) that draft sent 1,217 of one library's cells to Neoplastic cell and none to Glial cell, and renamed cells to
   tumour, lymphoid and pituitary names. An organoid grown from stem cells holds none of those. No label of that cohort
   can be quoted until a person approves a tree, and the draft is not one a person should be asked to approve.
2. **The leak guard.** scAnno's own files still hold cohort strings under the generated lists (the audit counted 36; a
   verifier saw 12 S1 lines listed on 2026-11-11, conform's listing stops at 25). While they stand, scAnno cannot count
   toward A3.1's leak half.

## The change
1. `tree propose` keeps the cell names the corpus files under the tissue's own lineages, and says which names it could
   not place and why, instead of drafting every lineage the tissue's studies mention. How "the tissue's own" is decided
   comes from the corpus (its tissue and lineage fields), not from a list written into scAnno, and not from either
   cohort (rule 1). A test that fails on `779fd08`, on a synthetic corpus.
2. A job in the second cohort's `stages/02_annotate/jobs/` that re-proposes its tree from the corpus job 01 already built
   (PBS 784414's sealed run), prediction in the header before any run (which of the draft's ten lineages stay, which go,
   and what happens to the names that were filed under the ones that go), submit recipe between `# SUBMIT-BEGIN` and
   `# SUBMIT-END`, a final `GATE:` line. The tree it writes is a DRAFT: no name goes into `approved_by`. Approving it is
   a person's act; the coordinator takes the re-proposed tree to the PI.
3. Every leak hit in scAnno's own files removed (the untruncated scan, with each cohort's terms and shapes): the string
   goes and the fact stays, cited by what the run established. No excuse added to any allow-list.

## Acceptance check
The tests fail on `779fd08` and pass on the branch; the suite green with a shadowing `tests` package; the untruncated
leak scan with both cohorts' terms and shapes finds nothing in scAnno; the fixture's axes still reach the pipeline; the
job reads `GATE: AS PREDICTED`. Rule 2: no assay rule changes. If `tree propose` is on the first cohort's promoted
stage-2 path, say what would differ there and write its reproduction; if it is not, say how you know.
