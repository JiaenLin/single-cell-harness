## Why
1. **GAP-C85.** `scanno tree propose` for Human / Brain drafts immune, tumour and endothelial lineages beside the neural
   ones and files "Proliferating cell" under B cell. On the second cohort (brain organoids, 53,534 cells after QC, PBS
   784490) that draft sent 1,217 of one library's cells to Neoplastic cell and none to Glial cell, and renamed cells to
   tumour, lymphoid and pituitary names. An organoid grown from stem cells holds none of those. No label of that cohort
   can be quoted until a person approves a tree, and the draft is not one a person should be asked to approve.
2. **The leak guard.** scAnno's own files still hold cohort strings under the generated lists (the audit counted 36; a
   verifier saw 12 S1 lines listed on 2026-11-11, conform's listing stops at 25). While they stand, scAnno cannot count
   toward A3.1's leak half.

## The change
1. `tree propose` keeps the cell names the corpus files under the tissue's own lineages, and says which names it could
   not place and why, instead of drafting every lineage the tissue's studies mention. How "the tissue's own" is decided
   comes from the corpus (its tissue and lineage fields), not from a list written into scAnno, and not from either
   cohort (rule 1). A test that fails on `779fd08`, on a synthetic corpus.
2. A job in the second cohort's `stages/02_annotate/jobs/` that re-proposes its tree from the corpus job 01 already built
   (PBS 784414's sealed run), prediction in the header before any run (which of the draft's ten lineages stay, which go,
   and what happens to the names that were filed under the ones that go), submit recipe between `# SUBMIT-BEGIN` and
   `# SUBMIT-END`, a final `GATE:` line. The tree it writes is a DRAFT: no name goes into `approved_by`. Approving it is
   a person's act; the coordinator takes the re-proposed tree to the PI.
3. Every leak hit in scAnno's own files removed (the untruncated scan, with each cohort's terms and shapes): the string
   goes and the fact stays, cited by what the run established. No excuse added to any allow-list.

### The change (fix round 1)
1. `scanno/propose.py`, the lineage tests counted in STUDIES (a study counts once however many markers it lists). A
   report is one study's report of a lineage in one of the context's tissues. It is counted from the rows a study
   reported (markers.py's `curated` sources), at any marker tier, so the evidence is about whether studies report the
   lineage, not how strong its markers are. A report is healthy when a row of it comes from a sample the corpus records
   as Normal. It is disease when a row comes from any other sample, and a study can be both. For lineage L in the
   context, with HT healthy and DT disease studies: (1) EVIDENCE H >= `min_studies` (default 2), so no lineage rests
   on one study; (2) SHARE H/HT >= `min_share` (default 0.02, one healthy study in fifty); (3) SPECIFICITY e >=
   `min_enrichment` (default 1.0), where the wide pool is the species' healthy reports, the context's included (not
   applied when the context reads every tissue); (4) DISEASE r < `disease_ratio` (default 2.0): a lineage that the
   context's disease studies report at least twice as often, as a share, as its healthy studies is the disease's.
   Names: the marker floor is unchanged. A name is placed under the kept lineage where the most healthy studies report
   it (then markers), when at least `min_studies` healthy studies report it there and it passes (4). Lineages are
   ranked by healthy studies and names by healthy studies then markers. The caps are unchanged.
2. Declared: the four are named defaults in propose.py, CLI flags (`--min-studies --min-share --min-enrichment
   --disease-ratio`), and values in the draft's `proposed.parameters`. `--lineage-tests off` drafts every lineage
   unchecked, and the draft says so.
3. When the corpus cannot tell, the refusal says why. Either the species has no healthy report at all (no row's
   disease reads Normal: a blank field or "Healthy" is not read as healthy), or the context has fewer healthy studies
   than `min_studies`. Each refusal names `--lineage-tests off`. When the context has fewer than 1/`min_share` healthy
   studies, the draft carries a `cannot_tell` line and stdout a REVIEW line: there, one study is more than the share.
4. Every kept, set-aside and not-placed entry carries `studies`, `healthy_studies`, `disease_studies`, `claims` and
   `healthy_claims`. Lineages also carry share, enrichment and disease ratio. `single_study` is true when one healthy
   study is all the evidence (reachable only with `--min-studies 1`). `approved_by` stays null.
5. Docs: README, QUICKSTART, USER_GUIDE and the CLI help say the rule as built, with no claim of what kind of lineage
   goes.
