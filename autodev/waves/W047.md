## Why
W035's two fix rounds closed the maker's bypass, the record and the validation rule; its last re-check failed on one
point: one cohort's run values are still in `kernels/cellchat.py`'s prose. They were there before W035, the builder's
scan missed them, and neither the leak guard nor the new warning can see them (GAP-C101). The same kind of value sits in
scProfile's host, which writes them by its own contract (GAP-C100). Rule 1: no cohort value in a tool.
