## Why
The largest block of points still under its bar sits on this line: A4.1 (0 of 9 plugins in the maker's layout passing
the suite), A4.3 (3 of 9 naming a peer-reviewed source and a pinned version), the last quarter of A3.2, and scProfile's
tool credit in A3.1 (its leak scan is red only on four held-out plugins' comments). The maker is the deliverable: a
held-out plugin is the maker's TEST, spent once, graded before the maker changes.

## The change, in this order
1. **Grade first, change nothing.** Convert `abundance` through the maker exactly as it stands on `main` (the blind
   conversion the repository's own protocol describes) and record what the maker gets right and wrong on it, before any
   change to the maker or to the plugin. That grade is this wave's first result and is written into the wave file.
2. **What the grade shows is fixed in the MAKER, never in the plugin by hand**, then the conversion is re-run. Every
   change to the maker is checked against the plugins already spent (`de`, `cellchat`): their output must not move.
3. **A source that exists (RULES item 18).** Through the maker's declaration verbs: `abundance` names its peer-reviewed
   source by DOI with the title and year its own record gives, and an exact pinned version of what it wraps; `de` gets
   its DOI and an exact upstream pin; `cellchat` its DOI. A check resolves each DOI and compares the recorded title and
   year; an offline test uses the recorded values. One cited claim per plugin is read against its source.
4. **The design (R32).** `abundance` fits or refuses, by name and with the reason, a two-factor design with interaction
   (today it fits one factor at a time): through the maker.
5. **The comment hits** that keep the leak scan red in `abundance`'s file go, through the maker's prose verb; the
   method's fact stays.
6. **The fixture reaches the tool (R46, GAP-054):** the fixture's dev point runs DE and abundance on the built
   environment and accepts no refusal as a pass.
7. **The cluster's dev suite** as the gate: a dated copy of the harness's `jobs/dev_suite.pbs` grading `de`, `cellchat`
   and `abundance`, with its prediction written before submission.

## Acceptance check
- The blind grade is in the wave file before any change, with what each later change to the maker answered.
- `abundance` is maker output (no hand edit: the record's check passes and a hand edit fails it), passes `sch dev` and
  `sch conform` on the workstation, and its design record is anchored as the repository's rule for a spent plugin says.
- Each of the three plugins' DOIs resolves to the recorded title and year; each pin is exact.
- The two spent plugins' outputs are byte-identical before and after every maker change.
- The leak scan's hits in `abundance` are gone; the other three held-out plugins' hits are untouched (their own waves).
- The dev-suite job reads AS PREDICTED at the head that merges.
