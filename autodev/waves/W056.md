  letter, a mark, a number, punctuation, a symbol). So no control character (NUL, CR, VT, FF, FS, GS, RS, U+0085, a
  TAB that is not the delimiter), no format character (U+FEFF, zero-width, direction marks), no line or paragraph
  separator (U+2028, U+2029), no space but U+0020 (U+00A0, U+3000), nothing unassigned;
- F3 it holds no `"`, no `'` and no `#` anywhere: the marks a table reader may read as a quote or a comment;
- F4 its HEADER decides the delimiter and holds one kind: TABs with no comma and no semicolon, or commas with no
  semicolon (F2 then excludes a TAB); a header with none is one column. In the BODY of a TAB table a comma or a
  semicolon is a plain character, and in a comma table a semicolon is;
- F5 it is rectangular: every line ends with LF, the last too; no line is empty; every line has the header's number of
  cells; no row is all empty cells;
- F6 no cell begins or ends with a space;
- F7 its header's names are not empty and are distinct with case ignored.

Its cells are then `text.split("\n")` and `line.split(delimiter)`: nothing is left for a parser to decide.
