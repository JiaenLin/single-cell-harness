# W059 (HARN): the cohort-run skill, and a cold agent's trial on the second cohort

## Why
GOALS A7.1: an agent with no prior context drives a cohort through the family with the skill alone: assemble, submit,
recover, audit, record. Nothing of it exists; the driver that makes it possible (one request, four stages, a generated
job) is in its last fix round. The sufficiency check found that without ONE planted failure, declared before the trial
starts, the "recover" act cannot be shown and the test stops at 4 of 5.

## The question the design must answer
What is "recover", as a CLOSED list: which failures the skill must tell apart (a pins miss, an absent input, a tool's
refusal, a kill at the time limit, a prediction that missed, and whatever else the driver's own verbs can report), the
ONE act allowed for each, and the rule that the request is the only thing the agent may change (never a tool, a job
file, a record or a threshold). And: through which command a cold agent submits and watches, given that compute runs
only inside submitted jobs and the agent must not need this program's private machinery. Answered wrongly, the skill
retries blindly, or meets a planted failure it cannot classify.

## What the design carries (item 21a)
The problem from the root; the design by construction (THE CLASS: the closed set of states a run can be in, the one
place that defines it, the one test that walks it); one other design and what it gives up; the steps as at most three
changes a wave (so: how many waves); the trial's protocol (what the cold agent is given, the planted failure and how
it is declared beforehand, what is graded for each of the five acts, who grades); the attacks the builder must run;
what the design gives up.
