# W062 (PLAT): the Studio says of a fact only what its source lets it say (wave S of W061's design)

**Registered** 2026-11-12 by the coordinator. The FIRST wave of the design in
[W061.md](W061.md), section "Design" (its wave S), which is this wave's contract: read it whole before any change.
ROUTE item R38's remainder; GAP-C132 (the Studio's part) and GAP-C129. **Goals** A6.2, PLAT.2, PLAT.3 (no score moves
by this wave alone). **Repository** scrnaseq-studio, branch `autodev/W062-facts-sentences`, from `main` (`e475703`,
deployed). **Designed and built by a Fable designer** (D-026); then one independent check; then its deploy (D-045).
It goes FIRST of the design's waves because bundles already in users' hands keep their unsourced fields: the Studio
must stop calling a guess "recorded" before the lab stops making the guess.

## Why
On the family's real object the Studio's generated Methods text says the data were corrected with one integration
method "as recorded in the object", with that method's citation, while the object records another; the field it reads
was guessed by the lab from an embedding's name. The Studio reads every non-null provenance field of a bundle as a
record. It also says pseudobulk counts "can be saved" where no raw counts are in the bundle (GAP-C129), and draws a
total that is not a total of counts under the label "Total counts".

## THE CLASS (from the design, section 2; the designer restates it against the code before any change)
Every sentence the Studio builds from a fact about the object (the Overview's facts, the generated Methods text and
each citation it picks, the QC labels, the pseudobulk sentences) has ONE source kind, taken from the bundle and never
from the look of a name: RECORDED (the bundle says where in the object it was read: then "as recorded" may be said, with
the place), MEASURED (said as measured, and from which matrix), or UNSOURCED (a bundle written before sources existed,
or a field with no source: said as what the bundle states, never as recorded, and no citation is chosen from it). The
ONE place that defines the sentences and their source kinds (the design names a sentence table); the ONE test that
walks it, with a decoy for every row (a name that looks like a method, a fact with no source).

## THE SIZE (three changes)
1. The sentence table and its walking test; every fact sentence and every citation choice goes through it.
2. Bundles as they exist today (no sources: every lab before the design's wave L1, and the offline exporters) are
   worded as UNSOURCED: nothing is called recorded, no citation is picked from a name; the Methods text says what was
   not recorded instead of guessing it. The words follow the Studio's written standard.
3. The counts sentences: "pseudobulk counts can be saved" only when raw counts are in the bundle (GAP-C129); a per-cell
   total is labelled as counts only when the bundle says it was summed from counts, else as what it is.

## Acceptance check

- The real object's shapes as SYNTHETIC bundles (no cohort value): two corrected embeddings with the recorded method

- Nothing else on any tab moves (screens before and after on one ordinary bundle); suite, lint, build green; the copy

- A PROOF TABLE in this file (`proof.py`: 0 incomplete).

### 2. How the Studio tells the kinds apart, FROM THE BUNDLE
One optional member, `meta.facts = { v: 1, <id>: Fact }`, in the design's shape, read by one closed reader in
`S:lib/facts.ts`. A fact is RECORDED only as `{kind:'recorded', value, at}` with a place; NOT RECORDED as
`{kind:'recorded', value:null, searched}`; MEASURED as `{kind:'measured', value, of, how}`; CHOSEN as
`{kind:'chosen', value, by}`. A bundle with no `facts`, or an id its `facts` does not hold, is UNSOURCED when the old
field states a value and NOT STATED when it states none. A fact the reader cannot take (an unknown kind, no `at`, a
value that is an object, markup, a line break, over 64 characters; a `facts` that is no object or not `v: 1`) is
UNREADABLE: said so, never drawn as recorded, and the old field is not used in its place. No kind is ever taken from
the look of a value, a key or a name. The ids are the Studio's now (the lab's schema does not exist yet: see 5):
`integration`, `integration_embedding`, `normalization`, `expression`, `counts` (with `carried` and `why`), `total`,
`genes_detected`, `mito`, `cell_type_column`, `embedding`, `not_called`, `gene_ids`, `doublets`, `ambient`.
