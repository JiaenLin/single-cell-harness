## Why
The maker is the deliverable and a held-out plugin is its TEST, spent once and graded before the maker changes
(RULES item 21a: a held-out plugin's blind grade comes BEFORE any design; the design follows the grade). `abundance`
was the first grade (W055); since then the maker gained a declared reference level, a source stage and a fixture tier
that accepts no refusal. `decoupler` is the second grade: it says whether those changes generalise to a plugin nobody
has converted, and it is the first of the three held-out plugins whose comments still keep the leak scan red for every
plugin (A4.1 cannot count while it is red). One plugin, one grade: a blind grade cannot be taken back.

## THE CLASS
A held-out plugin tells the truth about the maker only if the grade is taken with the maker and the plugin exactly as
they stand, is written down and pushed BEFORE anything changes, and every later change is made in the maker (or
through one of its verbs) and never in the plugin by hand. Whatever the grade shows that needs a maker change larger
than a line is NOT built here: it is handed to W067's design (the maker's decision sites), with the grade as evidence.

## THE SIZE (three changes, in this order)
1. **The blind grade, nothing changed.** Convert `decoupler` through the maker as both stand at `a2694ec` and the
   harness's `main` (the blind conversion the repository's own protocol describes; W055's "The blind grade" is the
   form). One row per maker command: what was run, what the maker said, right or wrong against what the plugin's code
   and its upstream library actually do (measured in a private environment built from the shipped lock). The section
   is written into this file and the branch pushed (an empty commit names it) before change 2 starts.
2. **Through the maker.** `decoupler` becomes maker output: the comment hits that keep the leak scan red in its file
   go through the maker's prose verb, the method's fact stays; its peer-reviewed source by DOI with the title and year
   its own record gives, and an exact pin of what it wraps, through the source stage. A maker defect the grade found
   is fixed here ONLY if it is a line or two and the two spent plugins' and `abundance`'s outputs do not move; any
   other is written as a row "for W067" and the conversion states what it could not do.
3. **The gate job.** A dated copy of the first cohort's `stages/04_profile/jobs/dev_suite_w065.pbs` grading `de`,
   `cellchat`, `abundance` and `decoupler`, its prediction written before any submission, with a CHECK_ONLY branch
   (BRIEFS 0b, A NEW JOB FILE). The coordinator submits it.
