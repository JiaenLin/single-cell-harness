# ADR-0021: the third turn - the author's twenty-two, the composed ratio's basis, the thread cap, and whether the number moves

**Date** 2026-09-14. **Follows** ADR-0020 (the loop converges). **Status** in progress; the table at
the end is filled as each step lands.

## Context

ADR-0020 closed with the loop's second turn run end to end on cold agents and its one number not
moved toward zero: 36 kinds surviving a fresh look became 25, owners HOST 3, PLUGIN 5, TOOL 17.
The three host kinds are answered in the mechanism (blind 0007 defects 2 and 4; the third was the
data's). The eight defects met were all in the reading of state and are fixed. Three things were
left open by name: the twenty-two kinds that are the plugin author's; the composed ratio's basis
(the host's two-scale table totals an arm over the pathways shared with its one comparison
partner, the plugin's `how_much_total` plate over the pool of all four arms, so the six composed
ratio claims were narrowed by a reviewer who checked every number); and the plugin's threads,
which burst to 59 cores on a share of 4, so `cores` cannot be declared until the plugin caps them.

## The goal, in one number

A third turn of the same loop in which the kinds surviving a fresh look go from 25 toward zero,
and the drop is the author's: more than the mechanism's own repairs account for. If the number
flattens instead, that is the finding, and the next round is about the worksheet's language.

## Decisions

1. **The composed ratio cites a plate on its own basis.** The sentence's number is the host's,
   over the shared elements of a pairwise contrast; it cites a plate that shows that number, or
   the host draws one, in the mechanism that composes and routes today. A check holds the number
   in the sentence against the plate it cites.
2. **The thread cap is a worksheet kind, not a hand edit.** The plugin passes the share the host
   gives it through to its upstream's workers; the author answers it like any other kind.
3. **The author's turn is the twenty-two kinds plus the cap**, from the worksheet the tool
   prints, in a cold room, pasted as before, every diff held against the cohort's vocabulary.
4. **The same loop, the same rules.** Emitted job, pinned node, lookers on the split, the audit,
   writer, reviewer, render, seal; predictions below committed before any change; a failed
   prediction is a finding, not a reason to rewrite it.

## Predictions, before any change

- **R1** the six composed ratio claims cite a plate on the shared basis, and a check that the
  sentence's totals appear on the cited plate fails on the composer as it is and passes after.
- **R2** on the next review, the composed ratio claims stand.
- **T1** the next run measures cellchat's one-second peak at or under twice its share, and the
  maker's status reads `cores` answered, written by the tool's own declare.
- **A1** the cold author answers all twenty-three kinds without a question, bumps the version
  once, and passes validate and the build status.
- **A2** after the rerun and the fresh look, at most eight kinds survive (25 now).
- **A3** no finding on the three host kinds of blind 0007.
- **S1** the writing seal holds W1-W3.
- **B1** the agents meet at most four defects of the mechanism, each fixed test-first the same day.

## What must not be done

No hand edit of `kernels/cellchat.py` except the paste of the author's answer; nothing to the
eight held-out plugins; no fix beside the mechanism that exists; no run on a login node; no
prediction rewritten after the numbers are in.
